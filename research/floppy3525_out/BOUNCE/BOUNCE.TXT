Bouncing Boubles is a Public Domain release from West Germany.  
The author is Paul Bininda.
     The object of the game is simple enough; Kill or be killed!  
To control your blaster ship, the <A> and <S> keys move it from 
side to side.  The <Spacebar> key fires.  If you want to use a 
joystick you can (but the keyboard is faster!).
